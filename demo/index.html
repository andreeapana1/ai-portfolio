Transfer Referral Assistant

At a money transfer counter, the clearest warning signs come from the conversation with the customer, not from the numbers. This demo reads a teller's notes, checks them against four warning signs from my own KYC/AML work, and drafts the referral to the compliance team. It compares a simple keyword check with an AI review.

The tool never decides. It only helps the teller write a clear referral. The teller stays calm and professional, and the compliance team decides whether to hold an additional interview. All cases are fictional.
